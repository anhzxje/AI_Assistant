# Personas

## Undergraduate Student

- Needs accurate answers about admissions, programs, schedules, tuition, scholarships, forms, and procedures.
- Values concise Vietnamese answers with citations to official HUS sources.
- Risk: may follow outdated announcements if freshness is not surfaced.

## Graduate Student

- Needs graduate program information, thesis/defense workflows, regulations, decisions, and advisor/lab context.
- Values source traceability and document version awareness.

## Lecturer / Researcher

- Needs quick lookup of departments, staff directories, program information, research/lab contacts, and administrative procedures.
- Values low hallucination and clear source provenance.

## Academic/Admin Staff

- Needs a maintainable knowledge base, QA reports, stale document detection, and feedback-driven improvements.
- Values dashboards, auditability, and data governance.
